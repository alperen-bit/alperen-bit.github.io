Push the Line — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Push the Line ("the game") is made by Alperen Korkmaz, an independent developer ("I", "me"). This page explains what data the game and the services it uses handle, why, and what you can do about it. Contact: support.alperen@gmail.com.

In short

  • The game is for players aged 13 and over. It asks your year of birth once; the year stays on your phone and only an age group (under 18 or adult) is passed to the ad services. If you are under 13, the game stops at a notice screen and nothing starts.
  • If you are 13 or over, the game creates an account for you automatically, backs up your progress and puts your best runs on a public leaderboard under a random name such as "Brave Medic 4821".
  • If you are 13 or over, the game sends gameplay statistics and crash reports, unless you turn this off in Settings → Send game data. In the EU, the UK and Switzerland this happens only if you are 18 or over and agree.
  • The game shows rewarded video ads from Google AdMob and Unity Ads. The ad services collect device data, even before you watch an ad.
  • You can delete your data from Settings → Account → Delete my data.

1. Data that stays on your phone

  • your year of birth (the answer to the age question); only the age group derived from it is shared, with the ad services (section 5). On iPhone, in regions where Apple shares an age range with apps (for example Texas), Apple may ask you to share it; if you do, the game stores the lowest age in that range as your year of birth and does not ask its own question;
  • your settings (sound, vibration, screen shake, language, reminders);
  • your game progress (best score, points, unlocked characters, daily multiplier); if you are 13 or over a copy is also backed up (section 2);
  • frame-time measurements the game uses to adjust its graphics quality. They are never sent anywhere.

Android's own backup of the game's files is turned off.

2. Account and cloud backup (13 and over)

After the age question, the game signs you in to Google Firebase, the game's server, automatically. If you are signed in to Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iPhone) on your phone, the account is linked to it. Otherwise it is an anonymous account. You don't enter a name, an email address or a password.

Stored on the server:

  • a random account ID created by Firebase;
  • if linked, your Play Games or Game Center player ID. The game does not use your Play Games or Game Center name or picture;
  • a backup of your progress (best score, points, unlocked and equipped characters, daily multiplier).

Why: so that your progress is not lost and, with Play Games or Game Center, comes back on a new phone; and so that leaderboard scores can be checked.

3. Leaderboard (13 and over)

When a run starts and ends, the game sends the run to the server: map, random seed, score, distance, kills, run time and app version. The server checks the run before it is listed.

  • Visible to everyone: a random name made of two words and a number, your country, your score and when it was set.
  • Your country and continent are worked out on the server from your IP address. The game's server code uses the IP address only for this and to limit abuse, and does not save it. Google's infrastructure keeps short-lived request logs, which include the IP address.
  • Run records are deleted after 30 days. Your leaderboard row stays until you delete your data.

4. Gameplay statistics and crash reports (13 and over)

The game uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the game is played and to fix crashes.

  • When: in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, only if you are 18 or over and have agreed in Google's consent form. Everywhere else, including Türkiye, it is on for players aged 13 and over. You can turn it off at any time in Settings → Send game data. Crash reporting stops from the next launch. Under 13: never.
  • What: game events (for example a run ending, how far you got and why you lost, unlocking a character, a purchase result, sharing a score, screens opened, the tutorial and reminder choices); device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version); an app-instance ID and a random install ID; approximate location from your IP address (worked out by Google); crash details (stack trace and app state).
  • Not collected: your advertising ID, your name, your email address or your precise location.
  • Retention: statistics are kept for 14 months. Crash reports are kept according to Google's Crashlytics retention.

5. Advertising

The game shows rewarded video ads: you can watch one to continue a run. Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google LLC), which also lets Unity Ads (Unity Technologies) bid to show the ad.

So that an ad is ready when you want it, the game loads one in the background when it starts. Ad services therefore receive data even if you never watch an ad:

  • on Android, your device's advertising ID and app set ID; on iPhone, the identifier for vendor and, only if you allow tracking (see below), the advertising identifier (IDFA);
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information;
  • ad interactions (ads loaded, shown, watched or tapped) and diagnostics.

Personalized ads:

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your permission first. You can change your choice in Settings → Privacy options. This row appears only where the form applies.
  • If you are under 18, or you are in Türkiye, the game asks the ad services not to personalize ads and to treat you with the protections for younger users.
  • On iPhone, Apple asks whether the game may track you (App Tracking Transparency). The IDFA is read only if you allow it. Players under 18, players in Türkiye and, in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, players who refused in Google's consent form are not asked, and their IDFA is never read. You can change this in your iPhone's settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

How these services use data: Google — policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, policies.google.com/privacy; Unity — unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

6. In-app purchases (13 and over)

Purchases are made through Google Play or the App Store. The game never sees your payment details.

  • Android: to confirm a purchase, the game sends Google's purchase token and the product ID to the game's server, which asks Google Play whether the purchase is valid.
  • iPhone: to confirm a purchase, the game sends Apple's signed transaction and the product ID to the game's server, which checks Apple's signature. Apple also notifies the server when a purchase is refunded or revoked, and the character is then removed.

The server keeps a record of each confirmed purchase (product, date, a store transaction reference and whether it was a test purchase; not linked to your account) so that one purchase can't be redeemed twice and a refunded purchase can't be restored.

7. Notifications

Daily-multiplier reminders are scheduled on your phone. Nothing is sent to a server. On Android 13 and later and on iPhone, the system asks your permission first, and you can turn reminders off in Settings → Reminders.

8. Feedback and sharing

  • Send feedback opens your own email app. The draft includes the app version, operating system, device model, language, your account ID (or "guest"), and your install ID if game data is on. You decide whether to send it. Emails reach a Gmail inbox.
  • Share creates a score picture on your phone and passes it to the app you choose.
  • Rating: after a good run the game may show Apple's or Google's own rating window. Your rating goes to the store; the game does not see it.

9. Children

The game is for players aged 13 and over. At first launch it asks the year of birth, which stays on the phone. If it shows a player under 13, the game stops at a notice screen: no account, ads, statistics, crash reports, reminders or purchases start, and nothing is sent anywhere. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents can contact me at support.alperen@gmail.com.

10. Where data is processed

The game's server functions run in the European Union (Belgium). The game's database is also in the European Union (Belgium and the Netherlands). Google, Apple and Unity may process data in other countries, including the United States, under their own safeguards.

11. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Settings → Account → Delete my data deletes, on your phone and on the server, your account, your cloud backup, your leaderboard row and your run records. It needs an internet connection. Afterwards the game starts again from the age question. Your ad consent choice and frame-time measurements stay on the phone until you uninstall.
  • An anonymous account with no online activity for 30 days is deleted automatically. An account linked to Play Games or Game Center is kept until you delete it.
  • Not removed by this button: statistics and crash reports already sent (they expire as in section 4); data held by the ad services (use the links in section 5); the purchase records in section 6, which are not linked to you; and short-lived server logs (they include your account ID and IP address).
  • Uninstalling the game deletes the data on your phone, but not the data on the server.

12. Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to the database is limited to each player's own records and to the game's server.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, or Türkiye's KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. Email support.alperen@gmail.com. If you include the account ID shown in your feedback email, I can find your records.

14. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Yaş sorusundan sonra oyun seni, oyunun sunucusu Google Firebase'de kendiliğinden oturum açtırır. Telefonunda Google Play Games'e (Android) ya da Game Center'a (iPhone) girmişsen hesap ona bağlanır, girmemişsen anonim bir hesaptır. Ad, e-posta adresi ya da şifre girmezsin.

  • Firebase'in ürettiği rastgele bir hesap kimliği;
  • bağlıysa Play Games ya da Game Center oyuncu kimliğin. Oyun Play Games ya da Game Center adını veya resmini kullanmaz;

Neden: ilerlemen kaybolmasın, Play Games ya da Game Center ile yeni telefonda geri gelsin; sıralamadaki puanlar denetlenebilsin.

Tur başlarken ve biterken oyun turu sunucuya gönderir: harita, rastgele tohum, puan, mesafe, öldürme sayısı, tur süresi ve uygulama sürümü. Sunucu turu listeye almadan önce denetler.

  • Herkesin gördüğü: iki kelime ve bir sayıdan oluşan rastgele bir ad, ülken, puanın ve ne zaman yapıldığı.

Türkiye dahil öteki her yerde 13 yaş ve üzeri oyuncularda açıktır. İstediğin zaman Ayarlar → Oyun verisi gönder ile kapatabilirsin; çökme raporu bir sonraki açılıştan itibaren durur. 13 yaş altında: hiçbir zaman.

Delete your account and data

In the game: open Settings → Account → Delete my data and confirm. This deletes your account, cloud backup, leaderboard row and run records from the server, and the game's data on your phone. An internet connection is needed.

Without the game (for example if you have uninstalled it): email support.alperen@gmail.com with the subject "Push the Line – delete my data". If you can, include the account ID shown in a feedback email from the game, or the leaderboard name you had. I will delete the same data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is kept and for how long is listed in section 11 above.

Hesabını ve verilerini sil

Oyunda: Ayarlar → Hesap → Verilerimi sil'i aç ve onayla. Bu, sunucudaki hesabını, bulut yedeğini, sıralama satırını ve tur kayıtlarını, telefonundaki oyun verisini siler. İnternet bağlantısı gerekir.

Oyun olmadan (örneğin oyunu kaldırdıysan): support.alperen@gmail.com adresine "Push the Line – verilerimi sil" konulu bir e-posta gönder. Yapabilirsen oyundan gelen bir geri bildirim e-postasındaki hesap kimliğini ya da sıralamadaki adını ekle. Aynı verileri 30 gün içinde siler, yanıtla bildiririm.

Neyin, ne kadar süre tutulduğu yukarıdaki 11. bölümde.
