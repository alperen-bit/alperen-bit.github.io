Suzu Pop! — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026 (rev. 3)

Suzu Pop! ("the game") is made by Alperen Korkmaz, an independent developer ("I", "me"). This page explains what data the game and the services it uses handle, why, and what you can do about it. Contact: support.alperen@gmail.com.

In short

  • The game is for players aged 13 and over. It has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own. You never enter a name, an email address or a password.
  • Your progress and settings are saved on your phone.
  • The game sends gameplay statistics and crash reports to Google Firebase. In the EU, the UK and Switzerland this happens only if you agree in Google's consent form.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob and Unity Ads: a small banner on the menu, and video ads you can choose to watch for a reward. Ad services collect device data, including your advertising ID.
  • Purchases go through Google Play. The game never sees your payment details.

1. Data that stays on your phone

  • your settings (music and sound volume, vibration, chosen music and colours);
  • your progress: best score, stars, unlocked outfits and backgrounds, daily puzzle results and streak, and purchased items;
  • the game in progress, so you can continue where you left off.

If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy your progress and the game in progress to your Google account backup, so they come back on a new phone. Nothing else from the game goes into that backup.

2. Gameplay statistics and crash reports

The game uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the game is played, to balance it and to fix crashes.

When: in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, only if you have agreed in Google's consent form (you can change your choice in Settings → Privacy choices). Everywhere else, including Türkiye, it is part of how the game works. If you don't want it, email me (section 9).

What:

  • game events: starting and finishing the tutorial, starting and ending a game (mode, result, score, number of trays, play time, parties, whether you continued, how many games you have played), the daily puzzle, opening the Wardrobe or the Shop, unlocking an outfit, and the result of a purchase;
  • purchases: Firebase records in-app purchases with the product ID, product name and price;
  • performance: your phone's model and the game's frame rate during a game;
  • device and app information collected by Firebase (operating system, language, app version) and an app-instance ID created by Firebase;
  • approximate location (country) worked out by Google from your IP address;
  • for crash reports: a random install ID created by the game, the screen and game mode at the time, the outfit and background in use, and crash details (stack trace and app state).

Not collected by these services: your advertising ID, your name, your email address or your precise location.

Retention: event data linked to the app-instance ID is deleted after 2 months; after that only aggregated statistics that are not linked to any ID remain (for example, how many players finished their first game). Crash reports are kept for 90 days.

3. Game settings from the server

Outside the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game downloads game settings from Google Firebase Remote Config at most twice a day, so the game can be tuned without an update. For this, Firebase receives an installation ID it creates, the app version and basic device information. No game events are sent with it.

4. Advertising

Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google LLC), which also lets Unity Ads (Unity Technologies) bid to show an ad. The game shows a small banner on the menu and, if you choose, a video ad to continue a game or to double the stars you earned. Players who buy "No Ads" see no ads.

Ad services receive:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information;
  • ad interactions (ads loaded, shown, watched or tapped) and diagnostics.

Personalized ads: in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your permission first. In US states with privacy laws, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your data in Settings → Privacy choices. The row appears only where these choices apply. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

How these services use data: Google — policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, policies.google.com/privacy; Unity — unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

5. In-app purchases

Purchases are made through Google Play. The game never sees your payment details. It checks Google's receipt on your phone and saves only which items you own; the receipt and the purchase token are not saved.

The purchasing library the game uses (Unity In-App Purchasing, Unity Technologies) sends purchase events to Unity: when a purchase starts, succeeds or fails, with the product, a transaction ID, basic device information and a session ID. Unity processes this data on the game's behalf; it is sent regardless of your ad consent choices, which only affect advertising identifiers.

6. Sharing

Share on the daily puzzle creates a short text with your result and passes it to the app you choose. Nothing is sent unless you send it.

7. Children

The game is for players aged 13 and over and is not designed for children. The game does not ask your age. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Parents can contact me at support.alperen@gmail.com.

8. Where data is processed

Google and Unity may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States, under their own safeguards.

9. Keeping and deleting your data, and your rights

The game has no account and does not show any ID to you, so I cannot link data held by Firebase or Unity to a specific player, and I cannot delete it on request. Instead, it is deleted automatically: event data after 2 months (only aggregated statistics without any ID remain) and crash reports after 90 days (section 2). Uninstalling the game deletes the data on your phone. Data held by the ad services: use the links in section 4.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, or Türkiye's KVKK), you may have the right to access or delete your data, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. Email support.alperen@gmail.com; I will reply within 30 days.

10. Security

The services above send data over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
